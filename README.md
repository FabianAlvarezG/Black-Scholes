## Options

According to Wikipedia, an option is a contract between a buyer and seller, where they agree on the possibility of executing a transaction of a specific amour of an asset, at a specific price at a specific time in the future. When the buyer has the possibility of buying (not obligation) is named a call option. When the seller has the possibility to sell, is referred as put options.
